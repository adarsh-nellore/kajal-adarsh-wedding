Andaz Room Block · Directions to Hotel

Breakfast: Provided at Andaz for hotel guests on Saturday, Sunday, and Monday mornings.

Transportation: All wedding shuttles leave from Andaz Condesa. If you stay elsewhere, please be within a short walk.

Airport: Please fly into MEX (Mexico City International Airport). MEX is approximately 20–45 minutes from the hotel, depending on traffic. Please keep in mind that the Welcome Event begins at 5 p.m. on Friday.

From Southern California: You may also consider flying from Tijuana (TIJ). Just keep in mind that crossing the border incurs some extra fees but is a great option.

Passports: Make sure your passport is valid for at least six months after your trip. International guests should check Mexico’s current entry and visa requirements for their nationality before booking.

Arriving: When you arrive, Uber is the easiest and most cost effective way to get to the hotel. If you prefer, we can help arrange private transportation to the airport.

Wedding Shuttles: All wedding shuttles leave from Andaz Condesa. If you stay elsewhere, please be within easy walking distance.

Getting Around: When you’re not at a wedding event, we highly recommend walking! Condesa, Roma, and many of our favorite spots are best explored on foot.

What should I know if I’m traveling internationally?

Please check Mexico’s current entry and visa requirements for your nationality before booking travel, and make sure your passport is valid for at least six months after your trip.

We also recommend:

  • Keeping a backup copy of your passport and important travel information
  • Keeping your phone charged while traveling
  • Letting your bank know you’ll be traveling internationally, if required
  • Giving yourself plenty of time at the airport

Additional guests can be accommodated for $30 per additional person per room per night, with a maximum of 3 adults or 2 adults + 2 children in double rooms.

I’m interested in staying at an Airbnb. What should I consider?

Of course! If you choose to stay at an Airbnb or another accommodation, we recommend choosing somewhere within easy walking distance of Andaz so you can easily access the wedding shuttles.

Also, be sure to confirm that your accommodation has air conditioning. Many Mexico City accommodations do not have A/C.

How many extra days should I stay in Mexico City?

We recommend staying for at least three extra days if you’d like to explore the city and surrounding areas. And if you have a week, you definitely won’t run out of things to do.

Some popular spots include:

  • Chapultepec Park: Museums, gardens, and a fortress
  • Coyoacán: Vibrant neighborhood that includes the Frida Kahlo Museum
  • Zócalo & Centro Histórico: Historic center, with tons of activity
  • Xochimilco: Colorful boats and canals
  • Teotihuacán: Aztec pyramids just outside Mexico City. Hot air balloons are popular!

Our favorite way to spend time in Mexico City is wandering around neighborhoods. Roma, Condesa, and Polanco are full of great restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, galleries, and bars.

We’ll add some of our favorite spots to the website as we get closer to the wedding!

How should I get from the airport to the hotel?

We recommend using Uber to get from the airport to Andaz. The app DiDi is another popular option.

If you’re not comfortable using a rideshare app, please let us know and we’ll be happy to help arrange transportation.

Should I exchange cash?

Most businesses, including market vendors, accept credit cards like Visa and Mastercard. However, if you would like to carry some Mexican pesos, the airport has the best exchange rates. There are some exchange places near the hotel as well.

What will the weather be like?

February is typically dry and comfortable in Mexico City, with warm days and cooler evenings. Expect daytime temperatures around 75°F and evenings around 60°F.

Mexico City is also at a high elevation, so you may notice the altitude when you first arrive. Take it easy on your first day, stay hydrated, wear sunscreen, and give yourself some time to adjust.

What should I know about safety in Mexico City?

Mexico City is vibrant and welcoming, but like any major city, a little awareness goes a long way. Use Uber or authorized taxis rather than hailing cars on the street, and keep your belongings secure in crowded areas.

For street food, look for busy stands with freshly prepared items, and stick to bottled water.
