A few passport reminders

If you’re traveling from the U.S., we recommend making sure your passport is valid for at least six months after your trip.

International guests should check Mexico’s current visa and entry requirements based on their nationality before booking travel.

Once you arrive, Uber is generally the easiest way to get around Mexico City. DiDi is also popular, and your hotel can help arrange private transportation if you prefer.

All wedding transportation to and from our events will leave from Andaz Condesa.

If you choose to stay elsewhere, we recommend staying within easy walking distance of Andaz so you can easily hop on the wedding shuttles.

And when you’re not at a wedding event, we highly recommend walking! Condesa, Roma, and many of our favorite spots are best explored on foot.

King rooms and suites accommodate 2 guests, while double rooms accommodate up to 3 guests. A rollaway bed may be available upon request.

Additional guests may be accommodated for a small nightly fee, with a maximum of 3 adults or 2 adults + 2 children in double rooms.

I’m interested in staying at an Airbnb. What should I consider?

Of course! If you choose to stay at an Airbnb or another accommodation, we recommend choosing somewhere within easy walking distance of Andaz so you can easily access the wedding shuttles.

Also, be sure to confirm that your accommodation has air conditioning. Many Mexico City accommodations do not have A/C.

What will the weather be like?

February is typically dry and comfortable in Mexico City, with warm days and cooler evenings. Expect daytime temperatures around 75°F and evenings around 60°F.

Mexico City is also at a high elevation, so you may notice the altitude when you first arrive. Take it easy on your first day, stay hydrated, wear sunscreen, and give yourself some time to adjust.

How many extra days should I stay in Mexico City?

We recommend staying for at least three extra days if you’d like to explore the city and surrounding areas. And if you have a week, you definitely won’t run out of things to do.

Some popular spots include:

  • Chapultepec Park: Museums, gardens, and a fortress
  • Coyoacán: Vibrant neighborhood that includes the Frida Kahlo Museum
  • Zócalo & Centro Histórico: Historic center, with tons of activity
  • Xochimilco: Colorful boats and canals
  • Teotihuacán: Aztec pyramids just outside Mexico City. Hot air balloons are popular!

Our favorite way to spend time in Mexico City is wandering around neighborhoods. Roma, Condesa, and Polanco are full of great restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, galleries, and bars.

We’ll add some of our favorite spots to the website as we get closer to the wedding!

What should I know about safety in Mexico City?

Mexico City is vibrant, exciting, and generally very welcoming - but like any major city, a little awareness goes a long way. Give yourself plenty of extra time to get around, as traffic can be unpredictable (especially during rush hours).

We recommend using Uber or authorized taxis rather than hailing cars on the street. Keep personal belongings secure, avoid flashy valuables, and be mindful in crowded areas.

When enjoying street food, look for busy stands with high turnover and freshly prepared items, and stick to bottled water. With a bit of planning and awareness, you’ll have an amazing experience exploring the city!

What should I know if I’m traveling internationally?

Please check Mexico’s current entry and visa requirements based on your nationality before traveling.

We also recommend:

  • Keeping a backup copy of your passport and important travel information
  • Keeping your phone charged while traveling
  • Letting your bank know you’ll be traveling internationally, if required
  • Giving yourself plenty of time at the airport

If you’re traveling from outside the U.S., please check the Mexican government’s current requirements for your country before your trip.
